QR Maker

An Android app that turns a document or a link into a QR code you can save or share.

What it does

  • Upload a file: pick a PDF, Word, Excel or PowerPoint file. QR Maker uploads it to your own Google Drive, makes it viewable by anyone with the link, and creates a QR code for that link.
  • Paste a link: paste any link and get a QR code straight away. No sign-in needed.
  • Save the QR code as a PNG to your phone's Downloads folder, or share it to WhatsApp and other apps.

How Google sign-in is used

QR Maker asks for one Google Drive permission: access to only the files that QR Maker itself uploads. It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Uploaded files go into a folder called QR Maker in your Google Drive. You can delete them there at any time.
