Privacy Policy

QR Maker for Android · Last updated 6 October 2026

Summary

QR Maker has no servers of its own. Your files go directly from your phone to your own Google Drive. The developer never receives your files, your links or your Google account details.

Information the app uses

  • Your Google account email address. Shown in the app so you know which account you are signed in with. It is stored only on your phone.
  • Files you choose to upload. Sent directly to your Google Drive, into a folder named "QR Maker". The app does not keep a copy after the upload finishes.
  • Links and file names you type. Used to draw the QR code. They are stored only on your phone so they are still there next time you open the app.
  • QR code images. Saved only when you tap Save, into your phone's Downloads folder, or sent only to the app you pick when you tap Share.

Google Drive access

QR Maker requests the drive.file permission, which only lets it work with files that QR Maker itself creates. It cannot see or change anything else in your Google Drive.

For each file you upload, QR Maker sets sharing to "Anyone with the link can view", because that is what makes the QR code work for the people who scan it. You can change or remove this sharing in Google Drive at any time.

QR Maker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location or other Drive files.

Removing access and data

  • Tap Sign out in the app to remove your Google sign-in from your phone and cancel the app's access.
  • You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Files you uploaded stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.
